Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my data safe offline?

A: Yes! All budget data stays on your device by default. No internet connection needed for core features. You can create budgets, track expenses, and review your spending completely offline.

Q: How do I recover my budget after reinstalling?

A: Recovery only works if you enabled backup before uninstalling:

  1. Reinstall Budget Buddy from the App Store
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Sign in with the same email you used before
  4. Tap "Restore purchases"
  5. Your data will download and appear in the app

Important: This only works if you had Premium and backup enabled. Without backup, reinstalling deletes local data.

Q: Do I need Premium for core features?

A: No! All core features are free:

  • Create budgets with categories
  • Track transactions
  • Manage bank account labels
  • Import/export data (CSV, Excel, PDF)
  • 12 app themes
  • 50+ currencies
  • 28 languages

Premium unlocks: Monthly check-ins and cloud backup (optional).

Q: Can I export my data?

A: Yes! Go to Settings → Data and export as:

  • CSV: For spreadsheet analysis
  • Excel: Formatted workbook with charts
  • PDF: For printing or sharing

This works whether you have Premium or not. Export anytime to keep a backup.

Q: How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

A: Cancel in the Apple Settings app (not in Budget Buddy):

  1. Open Settings → [Your Name]
  2. Tap Subscriptions
  3. Find "Budget Buddy"
  4. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

You'll keep Premium until your current billing period ends. Cloud backup remains available to download before cancellation.

Q: My purchase did not unlock Premium

A: Try restoring your purchase:

  1. Make sure you're signed in with the App Store account that made the purchase
  2. Open Settings → Account
  3. Tap "Restore purchases"
  4. Wait 10-15 seconds for the app to check with Apple's servers
  5. Premium badge should appear

If it still doesn't work, email us with your App Store receipt.

Q: My monthly check-in is locked

A: Monthly check-in is a Premium feature. To unlock:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap the Premium badge/button
  3. Choose monthly or yearly subscription
  4. Complete the purchase

If you already purchased and it's still locked, try Settings → Account → Restore purchases.

Q: Backup will not turn on

A: Cloud backup requires both Premium and a signed-in account:

  1. Verify you have Premium (check Settings for Premium badge)
  2. Verify you're signed in (Settings → Account shows your email)
  3. Toggle backup on (Settings → Cloud → Recovery backup)

If backup still won't enable after these steps, try signing out and back in again.

Troubleshooting Guide

If Budget Buddy crashes

  1. Force close the app (swipe up from bottom, hold)
  2. Wait 10 seconds
  3. Reopen the app
  4. If it keeps crashing, contact us with:
    • What you were doing when it crashed
    • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About)
    • Your iPhone model

If data seems missing

  1. Check you're viewing the correct month (swipe left/right on dashboard)
  2. Scroll down to see all transactions
  3. Try closing and reopening the app (force close)
  4. If data is still missing and you had backup enabled:
    • Sign out (Settings → Account → Sign out)
    • Sign back in
    • Your cloud backup will download

If export fails

  1. Make sure you have enough storage on your iPhone
  2. Try a different export format (CSV, Excel, or PDF)
  3. If you get an error, try exporting just the current month instead of all months
  4. Still having issues? let us know
